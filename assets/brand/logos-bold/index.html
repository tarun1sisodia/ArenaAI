Brand identity · Set B

Bold transport direction

SK Baghel Town & Travels. Heavy initials, a vehicle fused into the letters, tapered speed lines and the full name locked underneath — the language from the reference, drawn for SKB. Each concept comes in the brand navy + gold, a blue + orange transport colourway, a reversed cut for navy, and a compact cut without the name line.

Set A (the quieter, premium marks) is at ../logos/. Pick a direction and a colourway and I'll wire it through the header, footer, favicon and OG banner in both language trees.
