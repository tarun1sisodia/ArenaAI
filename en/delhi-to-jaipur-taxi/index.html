Route Guidance & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are toll taxes, state border permits, and fuel included in the Delhi to Jaipur fare?

Yes. All one-way taxi fares for Delhi to Jaipur (starting at ₹5,000 for Sedan) are 100% all-inclusive of toll plaza charges on the Yamuna Expressway/National Highways, state entry tax, and fuel. There are zero hidden surcharges at drop-off.

What happens if our train or flight arrives late for pickup?

We track live train and flight arrival schedules in real time. Your chauffeur will be waiting at the arrival terminal or station exit with a name-board. We never charge waiting penalties for train or flight delays.

Is there a night driving allowance for late departures?

For trips departing between 8:00 PM and 6:00 AM, a standard driver night allowance of ₹300 applies for cars and ₹500 for Tempo Travellers.
