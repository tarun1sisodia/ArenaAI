Agra Sightseeing & Monument Protocols

ASI Timings
event_busy Friday Taj Mahal Closure Warning

The Taj Mahal is strictly closed to general visitors every Friday for congregational prayers.

All other iconic Agra monuments operate 7 days a week: Agra Red Fort (06:00–18:00), Fatehpur Sikri (06:00–18:00), Mehtab Bagh (06:00–21:00), and Itmad-Ud-Daulah (Baby Taj). On Fridays, our chauffeurs curate the exclusive "Agra Beyond Taj" circuit.

For standard city sightseeing (8 Hours / 80 KM or Full Day), monument entry tickets are purchased by guests directly at ASI ticket counters or through the official ASI portal.

Designated ADA / ASI parking charges at monument compounds are paid at actuals, while fuel, car rental, and driver allowances remain fully covered by our package pricing.
