Highway Toll Governance

Fixed Expressway One-Way Toll Guarantee & Policy

verified

1. Cabs (Sedan, Ertiga, Innova Crysta) — 100% All-Inclusive

All confirmed one-way drops between Agra, Delhi IGI Airport, Noida, Gurgaon, Jaipur, and Mathura are strictly 100% all-inclusive. The quotation includes FASTag expressway electronic tolls (Yamuna Expressway, NH-19, NE-4, KMP), inter-state border passenger tax levies, and chauffeur daily driver allowances (Bhatta).

thumb_up Zero Empty-Return Surcharge: The guest is NEVER charged for the chauffeur's return drive to Agra.
info

2. Tempo Traveller & Force Urbania (Commercial Tourist Vans)

Under transport department regulations and interstate commercial vehicle operations, standalone one-way drops are commercially unviable as commercial tourist vans cannot secure unscheduled return passenger manifests on expressway corridors. Therefore:

  • All Tempo Traveller and Force Urbania bookings are billed on a round-trip baseline  or benchmarked to the standard 300 KM/day minimum schedule.
  • Where point-to-point one-way prices are explicitly cataloged (e.g., Delhi Drop at ₹8,500 for Tempo / ₹11,500 for Urbania), the tariff has already been pre-calculated to subsume the necessary two-way mobilization and fuel return empty run.
03
Round-Trip Distance Computation

Outstation Round-Trip 300 KM/Day Benchmark

Rule 3.1

Calendar Day Base

Multi-day outstation transit operates on a minimum average of 300 KM per calendar day (or actual cumulative KM logged on odometer, whichever is greater).

Rule 3.2

1.85× Same-Day Return

For same-day turnaround journeys on primary corridors (e.g. Agra ⇄ Delhi ⇄ Agra), the round-trip fare is computed at 1.85× of the single one-way rate.

Rule 3.3

Tolls & Parking at Actuals

On custom multi-day outstation routes, inter-state commercial taxes (Rajasthan, MP, Haryana) and monument parking slips are paid by guest at actual verified receipt.

calculate Representative Calculation Benchmark

Example Outstation Formula: A 3-day multi-city journey booked in an Ertiga SUV (₹14/km) carries a statutory minimum base of: 3 Days × 300 KM = 900 KM Baseline × ₹14/KM = ₹12,600 Base Transit Tariff. If the client logs 1,020 KM, the extra 120 KM is billed at ₹14/KM (₹1,680) upon itinerary closure.
