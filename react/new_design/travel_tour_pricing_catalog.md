## 5. Fare Calculations & Pricing Logic

### Fare Calculation Formulas

- **One-Way Outstation**: `Fare = Flat Route Rate OR (Distance KM * Per KM Rate)` (Tolls & State Taxes Included)
- **Round-Trip Outstation**: `Fare = Max(Round-Trip KM, Min Daily KM * Days) * Per KM Rate + (Driver Allowance * Days)`
- **1-Day / 120 KM Package**: `Fare = Base Package Price + Max(0, Actual KM - 120) * Extra KM Rate`
- **Tempo Traveller Group Hire**: `Fare = Max(Total KM, 300 KM * Days) * Per KM Rate + (Rs. 500 * Days Driver Charge) + Tolls + State Taxes + Parking`
- **Hourly Luxury (Audi)**: `Fare = (Hours * Rs. 190/hr) OR (Days * Rs. 360/day) + Fuel & Toll Surcharges`
