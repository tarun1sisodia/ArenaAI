Sunrise Journey Frequently Asked Questions

Crucial specifics about highway pickup timings, Taj Mahal gate ticketing protocols, and chauffeur greeting.

wb_sunny
Delhi Departure: 02:30 AM Reaches Taj East Gate precisely at first dawn glow
schedule Why depart Delhi at 02:30 AM?

The Yamuna Expressway takes approximately 3 hours from Central Delhi/Noida. Leaving at 2:30 AM guarantees arrival before 05:45 AM, bypassing long ticket queue bottlenecks and witnessing the marble transition from soft violet to fiery gold.

local_cafe Where is the palace breakfast served?

Following the morning Taj Mahal exploration (around 08:30 AM), your chauffeur escorts you to either ITC Mughal or Taj View hotel for an elaborate 5-star international and Indian culinary spread included in your voucher.

badge Can the chauffeur assist with luggage?

Yes, our chauffeurs provide white-glove curbside baggage assistance at your residence or airport terminal. Vehicles are equipped with luggage security locks and waterproof protective covers.

event_busy Is Taj Mahal closed on Fridays?

Yes, the Taj Mahal is strictly closed to public visitors every Friday for prayers. On Fridays, this tour automatically redirects to Fatehpur Sikri, Mehtab Bagh sunset view, and the Agra Red Fort Palace.
