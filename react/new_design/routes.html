Everything You Need To Know

Intercity Outstation Inquiries

Are toll taxes and expressway charges included in the one-way fares?

expand_more
Yes. All published one-way fares (e.g. Agra to Delhi NCR ₹3,499 on Dzire) explicitly include the Yamuna Expressway Fastag tolls, driver allowance, and doorstep delivery. You will never be asked for additional cash at toll plazas during a one-way trip.

Can the chauffeur pick us up directly from Delhi Airport (T3) or our Agra hotel at 3:00 AM?

expand_more
Absolutely. We specialize in 24×7 early morning departures and late-night airport pickups. For Indira Gandhi International Airport (Delhi T3, T2, or T1), our chauffeur arrives with an electronic or physical guest placard and tracks your flight number in real time.

How are round-trips calculated if we return the following day?

expand_more
Multi-day round-trips use the national tourist standard of 300 KM minimum per calendar day. For example, a 2-day trip to Jaipur covers up to 600 KM within the base rate. Any distance above 600 KM is billed at a fixed rate (e.g., ₹11/km for Dzire, ₹18/km for Innova Crysta). A driver night fee of ₹300 applies for overnight vehicle custody.

Can we make spontaneous stops for food or tea on the Yamuna Expressway?

expand_more
Yes, of course. All expressway routes include up to 30 minutes of complimentary rest stops at authorized highway plazas (such as the Mathura Expressway Oasis featuring Subway, Starbucks, Haldiram's, and immaculate washrooms). Just let your chauffeur know.

What happens if our inbound flight to Delhi is delayed?

expand_more
When booking an airport transfer, you supply your flight number. Our Agra dispatch hub monitors live radar. In the event of flight delays, our driver adjusts their arrival at Delhi T3 accordingly, with 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time from touch-down.
