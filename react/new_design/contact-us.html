Frequently Addressed Inquiries

24×7 Agra Dispatch Questions Answered

Can I book a cab at 2:00 AM or 4:00 AM for early morning Taj Mahal sunrise or Delhi Airport drops? expand_more
Yes, absolutely. Our Taj Ganj garage and telephone desk run 24 hours continuously. Early morning Taj Mahal sunrise tours (typically 5:00 AM pickup) and late-night Yamuna Expressway transfers to Delhi IGI Airport are routine operations. We recommend locking these 6 hours in advance, though emergency calls are dispatched within 20 minutes across Agra city.
How quickly can a cab arrive if I need an immediate pickup in Agra? expand_more
For locations within Taj Ganj, Fatehabad Road, and Agra Cantt Railway Station, our average driver arrival time is between 12 to 20 minutes from confirmed call. If you are stranded along the Yamuna Expressway near the Agra Toll Plaza, our designated highway patrol cab can reach you within 25 minutes.
What information will I receive once my booking is confirmed? expand_more
Upon token advance payment (28%), you receive an official WhatsApp and Email voucher containing: Vehicle Registration Number, Model & Color, Chauffeur Full Name, Police Verification ID, Driver Direct WhatsApp/Phone, and a live GPS link shared 30 minutes prior to departure.
What happens if my train (Gatimaan Express) or flight to Delhi IGI is delayed? expand_more
We actively monitor train arrival statuses on RailYatri/NTES for trains such as Gatimaan Express (12050/12049) and Vande Bharat, as well as flight tracking for Delhi IGI arrivals. We provide up to 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time without penalty so your driver is right at the exit gate when you step out.
What is the cancellation and refund policy for advance deposits? expand_more
We offer 100% full refund on the 28% advance deposit if cancellation is notified 24 hours prior to scheduled pickup time via call or WhatsApp. Cancellations made between 12 to 24 hours can be converted into 100% travel credit valid for 12 months with zero rescheduling fees.
